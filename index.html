?Before you book

Fair questions, straight answers.

Why not hire an SDR?

A new SDR only produces with a target list, a message and a process to step into. We build those first, and can coach the hire.

Why not buy a tool, like Clay or an AI SDR?

A tool sends whatever you give it. Without the right accounts and a reason to talk to each one, it sends more of the wrong email, faster. We decide who and why, then use the tools.

We tried outbound. It didn't work.

Usually the list or the reason to reach out was wrong, not the channel. The pipeline review shows which.

Will this put our domain at risk?

No. Cold email runs from separate sending domains, warmed up, with bounce and spam limits. Never from your main domain.
