The Problem

Most B2B founders build a great product.
Few build the sales engine to sell it.

Hiring a VP of Sales feels too early. Generic agencies feel too removed. And doing it alone means the pipeline stalls while the product waits. There is a better way — an embedded partner who builds the engine and runs it alongside you.
