Start with an audit

GTM Audit

Find exactly what's broken.

A two-week diagnostic of your commercial motion: what works, what blocks growth, and what to fix first. Not a slide deck, but a working document you can execute from day one.

What we audit

  • ICP definition and targeting logic
  • Messaging and value proposition clarity
  • Outbound sequences and copy
  • CRM structure and pipeline hygiene
  • Toolstack and workflow efficiency
  • Sales process and follow-up discipline
  • Conversion bottlenecks at each stage

What you receive

  • Full written diagnostic report
  • Prioritised action plan with quick wins
  • Rewritten messaging and sequence recommendations
  • ICP refinement with targeting criteria
  • CRM and toolstack recommendations
  • 90-minute implementation session
  • 30-day follow-up check-in

Process

Week 1Discovery calls, CRM access, sequence review, call shadowing and objection mapping
Week 2Diagnostic write-up, prioritisation, delivery session and action plan walkthrough
+30 daysCheck-in included

Right for you if

You're a B2B founder with a product that works but a pipeline that doesn't. You're doing outbound but not converting, a sales hire isn't hitting numbers, or you're about to invest in growth and want to know exactly where to start.

Build the engine

Pipeline Sprint

Build the engine. Then run it.

We design your entire outbound system from scratch, then run it week by week alongside your team until it produces consistent results. By the end, you have a working engine, a coached team, and the playbooks to keep it running without us.

What we build

  • ICP definition and account targeting lists
  • Multi-touch outbound sequences (email + LinkedIn)
  • Toolstack setup and CRM configuration
  • Objection-handling frameworks
  • Call scripts and discovery frameworks
  • Pipeline reporting and KPI dashboard
  • Full outbound playbook on handoff

Ongoing support

  • Weekly pipeline review calls
  • SDR coaching and live call feedback
  • Sequence iteration based on reply data
  • Prospect list management and enrichment
  • CRM hygiene and pipeline progression
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Direct Slack access throughout

Process

Month 1ICP, sequences, toolstack, first campaigns live
Month 2SDR coaching, iteration, pipeline building
Month 3Optimisation, reporting, playbook handoff

Right for you if

You have a product with clear market fit but no systematic outbound motion. You may have an SDR or small sales team without clear direction, and you're ready to invest three months in something that outlasts the engagement.
