GTM Audit

Find exactly
what's broken.

Two weeks Flat fee Remote or on-site

Most founders know something is wrong with their pipeline — they just don't know exactly what, or in what order to fix it. The GTM Audit cuts through that ambiguity.

Over two weeks, we run a full diagnostic of your commercial motion: your messaging, your ICP, your sequences, your toolstack, and the way your team (or just you) runs outbound. We map what's working, what's blocking, and what to do first.

You walk away with a prioritised action plan and a dedicated implementation session — not a slide deck, but a working document you can execute from day one.

What we audit

  • ICP definition and targeting logic
  • Messaging and value proposition clarity
  • Outbound sequences and copy
  • CRM structure and pipeline hygiene
  • Toolstack and workflow efficiency
  • Sales process and follow-up discipline
  • Conversion bottlenecks at each stage

What you receive

  • Full written diagnostic report
  • Prioritised action plan with quick wins
  • Rewritten messaging and sequence recommendations
  • ICP refinement with targeting criteria
  • CRM and toolstack recommendations
  • 90-minute implementation session
  • 30-day follow-up check-in

How it works

  • Week 1 — discovery calls, CRM access, sequence review
  • Week 1 — shadowing calls and objection mapping
  • Week 2 — diagnostic write-up and prioritisation
  • Week 2 — delivery session and action plan walkthrough
  • Post-engagement — 30-day check-in included

Right for you if

You're a B2B founder with a product that works but a pipeline that doesn't. You're doing outbound but not converting. You have a sales hire who isn't hitting numbers. Or you're about to invest in growth and want to know exactly where to start.

Pipeline Sprint

Build the engine.
Then run it.

Three months Monthly retainer Fully embedded

The Pipeline Sprint is for founders who are ready to build a proper outbound motion — and want someone to build it with them, not just advise on it.

Over three months, we design your entire outbound system from scratch: ICP definition, sequence copywriting, toolstack setup, SDR coaching, and weekly pipeline reporting. We don't hand it over and leave — we run the motion week by week alongside your team until it produces consistent results.

By the end, you have a working outbound engine, a coached team, and the playbooks to keep it running without us.

What we build

  • ICP definition and account targeting lists
  • Multi-touch outbound sequences (email + LinkedIn)
  • Toolstack setup and CRM configuration
  • Objection-handling frameworks
  • Call scripts and discovery frameworks
  • Pipeline reporting and KPI dashboard
  • Full outbound playbook on handoff

Ongoing support

  • Weekly pipeline review calls
  • SDR coaching and live call feedback
  • Sequence iteration based on reply data
  • Prospect list management and enrichment
  • CRM hygiene and pipeline progression
  • Monthly performance reporting
  • Direct Slack access throughout

How it works

  • Month 1 — ICP, sequences, toolstack, first campaigns live
  • Month 2 — SDR coaching, iteration, pipeline building
  • Month 3 — optimisation, reporting, playbook handoff
  • Weekly check-ins throughout all three months
  • Option to extend or transition to Revenue Partner

Right for you if

You have a product with clear market fit but no systematic outbound motion. You may have an SDR or small sales team without clear direction. You're ready to invest three months in building something that outlasts the engagement — not just generating a few leads.
