Privacy, kept simple.

Who we are

Nima GTM is the trading name of Alexis Naraghi, a sole trader based in London, UK. For anything about your personal data, write to alex@nimagtm.com.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and uses no analytics or advertising tools. Fonts and images are served from this site, so no third party is contacted when you browse it.

What we receive, and why

If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it to reply and, if we work together, to run the engagement.

If you book a call, the booking page is run by Google Calendar. Google receives the details you enter, under its own privacy policy, and we receive them to hold the meeting.

When you visit this site, our host, Netlify, keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser and pages requested) to run and protect the website.

Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and running our business, or taking steps towards a contract you ask for.

Sharing and storage

We don't sell your data. We share it only with the providers above (Google and Netlify), which may process it outside the UK under their own safeguards. We keep enquiries only as long as needed for the reason you contacted us, or as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by writing to alex@nimagtm.com. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
