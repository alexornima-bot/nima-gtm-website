Pipeline SprintSDR ManagementCRM EnrichmentOutbound

Everything broken. Everything rebuilt.

Stan Amsellem's company NextLevel helps tech teams turn legacy processes into AI-native operations. The offer was strong. The sales motion was not. There was no single thing to fix, so we rebuilt it from zero.

The situation

  • No outbound motion — zero systematic prospecting in place
  • No follow-up system — leads going cold with no reactivation
  • CRM in disarray — no structure, no pipeline visibility
  • ICP not clearly defined — targeting too broad to convert

What we did

  1. SDR management — day-to-day coaching and accountability
  2. Full CRM enrichment on Attio — rebuilt pipeline architecture and contact data
  3. Built dormant lead lists and launched structured reactivation campaigns
  4. Redefined ICP and outbound targeting to sharpen conversion
  5. Introduced NextLevel to the LVMH Group, opening active discussions
